    About Us

    Patson Machines Private Limited, being a top Manufacturer & Exporter of SPM Machine, CNC Machine, Multi Spindle Machine, Drilling Machine and other heavy-duty machines which conform to OEM standard in terms of overall quality, has been for the last 25 years serving Indian as well as international clients who prefer our various range of machines which feature frugal operational costs, maximum up time and less maintenance requirement. As our long experience, better expertise, well-equipped infrastructure and proficient engineers empower us to develop technology related to Multi Spindle Heads which prove to be functionally versatile for drilling and tapping holes, so we have been delivery what exactly we promise.
