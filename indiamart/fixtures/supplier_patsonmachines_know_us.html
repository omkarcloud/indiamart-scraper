    Patson Machines Private Limited

    Patson Machines Private Limited, being a top Manufacturer & Exporter of SPM Machine, CNC Machine, Multi Spindle Machine, Drilling Machine and other heavy-duty machines which conform to OEM standard in terms of overall quality, has been for the last 25 years serving Indian as well as international clients who prefer our various range of machines which feature frugal operational costs, maximum up time and less maintenance requirement. We design Multi Spindle Machines in different configurations with the higher functional efficiency and optimum drilling operation. As our long experience, better expertise, well-equipped infrastructure and proficient engineers empower us to develop technology related to Multi Spindle Heads which prove to be functionally versatile for drilling and tapping holes, so we have been delivery what exactly we promise.

    Patson Machines Private Limited is engaged in development of special purpose machines and automation. Its founder director Mr. S. N. Patwardhan has vast experience, having worked in cmti, bangalore and pmt machine tools automatics, pune in senior positions in design and development.he has designed and developed several machines for ordnance factories. special purpose machines also develpoed for auto ancillary and engineering industries. our machines have helped our customers to reduce machining cost per piece, increase output and reduced reliance on costly equipment like VMCs without any compromise on quality.

    About Directors

    Mr. S. N. Patwardhan, Director of Company is in the field of Machine Tool and Automation since 1972. After completion of M.Tech from IIT Mumbai, he worked in Central Machine Tool Institute, Bangalore for 7 years. Later on he worked as incharge of Design and Development of PMT Machine Tools, Pimpri, Pune for 10 years.

    A Graduate in Electrical Engineering, Mr. A. S. Patwardhan is in field of Machine tool and Automation since 1998. He has hand on vast experience on several projects with different Electrical and PLC control.
