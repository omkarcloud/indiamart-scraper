    Risansi Industries Limited

    "Risansi Industries Limited", headquartered in Kanpur. India., Manufacturer and Exporter markets progressive Pump & Valves for delivering low to highly viscous, aggressive and abrasive media With a dense network of subsidiaries. Branch offices and agencies we are currently being represented successfully in more than 10 countries.

    For more than 30 years. we have been offering optimum, economical solutions based on low life-cycle costs, to our customers in the chemical. food, paper. petroleum. mining, sugar and distillery numerous other industries. India gives you an advantage of low cost, technological advancement through our in-house research and development by experienced engineers resulting in innovative product designs that differentiates RISANSI from the competition. Continual improvement of our products based on customer feedback is our policy. We will continue to expand our leading market position in the future by developing innovative and market-driven products and services.

    About Directors

    • Risansi Industries Ltd. Formerly known as Pumps India Highflow Brand
    • Mr. Vipul Agarwal founded the company in 1979 after graduating as a Metallurgical Engineer (B.Tech) from Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur. He was the first to introduce ‘Progressive Cavity Pumps’ in the Sugar industry in India. And we command 90% of business as of date.
    • With a technological bent of mind and 200 years of family history as industrialists in various sectors such as manufacturing of ice, textile, flour and cold chain businesses, Mr.Agarwal founded Pumps India. His strong belief in continual research and development and in-house manufacturing to maintain premium quality of products has lead to the growth of the company, satisfied list of clients and engaged employees.
    • Mr. Agarwal promotes technological innovation, integrity and cost effectiveness. With his vision, the home grown company now runs as a promising professional organisation.
